## About Me

I am an independent researcher specializing in Arabic literature, classical poetry, literary history, and Islamic heritage.

My research interests include the study of Arabic literary traditions, critical approaches to classical texts, biographical literature, and the preservation and study of manuscripts.

I am particularly interested in the relationship between literary heritage, historical criticism, and contemporary scholarly research.

## Research Interests

- Arabic Literature and Literary Criticism
- Classical Arabic Poetry
- History of Arabic Literary Traditions
- Islamic Heritage and Maliki Biographical Literature
- Manuscript Studies and Textual Research
- Arabic Language and Philological Studies

## Research Projects

- **Ṭabaqāt al-Mālikiyya:** A research project examining Maliki biographical literature, scholarly classification, and hadith-critical methodology.
- **Critical Editing of Tārīkh al-Jabartī:** A scholarly project focused on the study and critical editing of historical sources.
